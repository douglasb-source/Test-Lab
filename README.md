About Us Test Lab
Located at the heart of the South East Technological University (SETU) Carlow campus, the Interaction Futures Lab is a dedicated research and design hub where the boundary between humanity and technology is redefined.

As we move toward an era of ubiquitous AI and complex digital systems, our mission is simple but profound: to humanise technology.

Our Vision
We believe that "cutting-edge" should not mean "complicated." Our lab serves as a sandbox for the future, exploring how advanced technology—from ambient sensors in smart homes to AI-driven medical diagnostics—can be designed to feel intuitive, inclusive, and deeply human-centered.

What We Do
The Interaction Futures Lab sits at the intersection of High-Tech and Human-Touch. We provide a world-class user-testing environment to explore:

Advanced Inclusive Medical Care: Designing medical devices that prioritize the dignity and capability of the patient, ensuring technology assists rather than intimidates.

Smart Homes for Independent Living: Developing sensing environments for older populations that provide safety and connectivity without sacrificing privacy or comfort.

AI & Cognitive Interaction: Investigating how humans trust, communicate with, and rely on Artificial Intelligence in high-stakes environments.

Research at SETU Carlow
As part of the SETU ecosystem, we bridge the gap between academic rigor and industrial application. Our space is designed for:

Iterative Prototyping: Rapidly building and breaking ideas to find the most ergonomic and cognitive-friendly solutions.

User-in-the-Loop Testing: Engaging real-world participants—from healthcare professionals to older adults—to ensure our designs work in the wild, not just the lab.

Cross-Industry Collaboration: Partnering with medical device manufacturers and tech innovators to bring inclusive design to the global market.

"We don't just design for the future; we design for the people who will live there."
